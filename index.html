2. Information We Collect & Why

We collect only the minimum information required to provide secure cloud-synced bookkeeping and EMI tracking:

A. Account & Authentication Information

  • Google Sign-In Profile: Your name, email address, and unique Firebase User ID (UID) obtained through Android Credential Manager and Google Firebase Authentication to sign you in securely and isolate your private ledger.

B. User-Entered Bookkeeping & Ledger Records

  • Khata, Borrower & EMI Records: Borrower names, contact notes, principal/EMI schedules, wallet entries, collateral notes, staff notes, and personal taken-loan trackers that you explicitly type into the app.
  • Customer Portal Sharing: If you link a borrower's email address to a loan record, that borrower can sign in with that exact email address to view read-only installment status and download receipts/statements associated with their linked loan.

C. Optional User-Selected Photos & Documents

  • KYC / Document Attachments & Digital Signatures: Images or PDFs you explicitly select via Android's zero-permission Photo Picker or capture using your device Camera when attaching records to a ledger entry. We never scan your photo gallery or device storage in the background.

D. App Diagnostics, Push Tokens & Advertising Identifiers

  • Notification Token: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token used solely to deliver EMI due reminders and account alerts you enable.
  • Advertising ID (Free Version): Google AdMob may process your Android Advertising ID and coarse diagnostic signals to serve advertisements in compliance with Google's privacy policies.

3. Android Permissions We Use (Least-Privilege)

Riso EMI Manager follows Google Play's strict permission minimization guidelines:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required for Google Sign-In, Firebase Cloud Firestore sync, and displaying AdMob ads.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS & RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED & VIBRATE: Used only to show your scheduled EMI due reminders and daily alerts after device restarts.
  • CAMERA (Optional, runtime-requested): Requested only when you explicitly tap to capture a document photo inside the app. You may deny it and still use the entire app.

Permissions We Do NOT Request: We do not request READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_CONTACTS, READ_SMS, SEND_SMS, READ_CALL_LOG, or ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION. Features like WhatsApp reminders, SMS reminders, phone dialing, and email support simply launch your phone's native app when you tap a button.

5. Data Security, Storage & Retention

  • Strict Access Control: All cloud data is protected by authenticated Firebase Security Rules so only your signed-in account (and explicitly email-linked borrowers for their own read-only loan view) can access records.
  • Encryption: All data transmitted between your Android device and Google Firebase servers is encrypted using modern TLS/HTTPS protocols.
  • On-Device Privacy: You can optionally enable Fingerprint / Biometric & 4-digit PIN App Lock inside Settings.
  • Retention: We retain your cloud ledger records only while your account remains active. When you delete your account, your records are permanently erased.

6. How to Delete Your Account & Data

You have complete control over your data and can permanently delete your Riso EMI Manager account and all associated cloud data at any time using either method below:

Method 1 — Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

  1. Open Riso EMI Manager on your Android device.
  2. Go to the Settings tab.
  3. Scroll to Help & Account and tap Manage account.
  4. Tap Confirm Delete to permanently erase your cloud ledger records and sign out.

Method 2 — Request Deletion via Email (Web / Without App)

If you have uninstalled the app, email us at risofinance@gmail.com from your registered Google email address with the subject "Account & Data Deletion Request — Riso EMI Manager". We will verify your request and permanently delete all associated cloud data within 7 business days.

What Data Is Deleted

Upon deletion, your user profile, borrowers, khata entries, loan schedules, wallet transactions, staff records, locker notes, and uploaded cloud documents stored in Firebase Firestore are permanently removed and cannot be recovered. Local files you previously exported to your phone's Downloads folder (such as PDF receipts or CSV reports) remain on your device under your direct control until you delete them manually.
